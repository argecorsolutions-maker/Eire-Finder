What Eire Finder is

Eire Finder is a browser extension that overlays official Property Price Register sale history, market trend data and related public information onto property listings on Daft.ie, MyHome.ie and Property.ie. Some information (BER, price, floor area) is read directly from the listing itself; other information is matched automatically against official and public data sources.

Accuracy — please read this one

Address matching against the Property Price Register, and every other figure the extension shows, is done automatically and is not manually verified for each property. Matches can occasionally be wrong, missing, or based on address normalisation that doesn't perfectly resolve an ambiguous or unusual address. Market trend, estimated value, rental yield and every other calculated figure are estimates built from regional data, not a valuation of the specific property. Nothing in the extension is financial, legal or professional advice, and none of it replaces your own due diligence, a solicitor, or a qualified valuer before making a property decision.
