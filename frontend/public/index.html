01 — The problem

The advert tells you half the story.

People make six-figure decisions on Irish property with information that is technically public — but scattered across records no advert will gather for you.

01

Asking price isn’t selling price

What a property last sold for — and when — is public record in Ireland. Almost nobody checks it before making an offer.

02

Time on market is hidden

Listings routinely sit for over a year. That is negotiating leverage the advert never mentions — and never will.

03

One price, no context

Registered sales on the same street tell you what the market actually pays. Nothing puts them beside the advert.

None of this is secret. All of it is scattered.

What Eire Finder is

Not another property portal.

Eire Finder lists no properties and competes with no one. It is an intelligence layer that runs on top of the adverts you are already viewing. There is nothing for estate agents to adopt, no new website to check — it works from the day you install it.

04 — FAQ

Fair questions.

Where does the data come from?

Primarily the Property Price Register — the official Irish government record of residential property sales. Sale prices and dates are matched from that register automatically. True time on market comes from the listing itself, and street comparables are other registered sales on the same road, organised so you can read the local market at a glance.

Which sites does it work on?

Daft.ie, MyHome.ie and Property.ie — together they cover the vast majority of property adverts in Ireland. Support for further sites is on the roadmap.

Is Eire Finder affiliated with Daft, MyHome, Property.ie or the Irish government?

No. Eire Finder is a fully independent product with no affiliation to any property portal, estate agent or State body. We simply read public records that anyone can consult, and put them beside the advert — at the moment you actually need them.

Will the extension track my browsing?

No. Eire Finder activates only on supported property sites, and nothing about your browsing history leaves your browser.

Why isn’t it free?

Accurate, automatic matching against official records takes continuous work. €1.99 a month keeps Eire Finder independent, ad-free and maintained — a small fraction of the cost of the decision it supports.
